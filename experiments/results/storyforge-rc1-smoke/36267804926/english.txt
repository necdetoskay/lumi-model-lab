The Red Chalk Bridge

The sun was warm on the courtyard pavement. Deniz, who was four years old, sat on the ground with a big piece of blue chalk.

Scribble, scribble, scribble!

Deniz drew a long, long blue road. "Vroom, vroom!" said Deniz. "My road is for fast cars!"

A little distance away, Ada was drawing too. Ada had a yellow piece of chalk.

Scribble, scribble, scribble!

Ada drew a long, long yellow road. "Beep, beep!" said Ada. "My road is for buses!"

Deniz looked at the blue road. It was very nice. But Deniz wanted to draw a house next to it. A red house!

Deniz looked in the chalk box.

Rattle, rattle.

There was only one little piece of chalk left. It was a tiny piece of red chalk. It was very short, like a little red crayon stub.

Just then, Ada finished drawing a sun. Ada wanted to draw a red flower next. Ada looked in the chalk box too.

"Oh!" said Ada.

"Oh!" said Deniz.

They both saw the red chalk at the same time. They both reached for it at the same time!

Grab!

Deniz held one end. Ada held the other end.

"I need the red chalk!" said Deniz. "I want to draw a red house!"

"I need the red chalk too!" said Ada. "I want to draw a red flower!"

They both held on tight. They did not let go. They looked at each other.

Then, they stopped pulling. They were very still.

Deniz looked at the blue road.

Ada looked at the yellow road.

There was a big empty space between the blue road and the yellow road. It was just grey pavement.

Ada looked at the space. Then Ada looked at Deniz. Ada had an idea!

"Look!" said Ada. She pointed at the empty space. "The roads are far apart. What if we use the red chalk to draw a bridge? A bridge can join them!"

Deniz looked at the space. A bridge! That was a great idea!

"Yes!" said Deniz. "A red bridge! Then my car can drive to your bus!"

Deniz and Ada held the little red chalk together. Deniz's hand was on one side. Ada's hand was on the other side.

They pushed the chalk down on the pavement.

Scribble, scribble, scribble!

They drew a big arc going up, up, up, and then down, down, down. It was a beautiful red bridge! It touched the blue road on one side and the yellow road on the other side.

Now the roads were connected!

Deniz and Ada stood up. They brushed the dust off their knees.

They looked at their drawing. The blue road went this way. The yellow road went that way. And right in the middle, the red bridge held them together.

"We did it!" shouted Deniz.

"We made a bridge!" shouted Ada.

"Vroom, vroom!" said Deniz.

"Beep, beep!" said Ada.

And they cheered together, "Hooray!"
