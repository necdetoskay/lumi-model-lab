# Deniz and the Muddy Box Swamp

Deniz was sitting on the floor at home. He had his toy dinosaur in his hand. Up, up went the dinosaur, high over Deniz's head. Zoom! It swooped down low, right past his toes. What a fun game!

"Roar!" said Deniz. "I am a big, strong dinosaur!"

Then Deniz saw something next to him. It was an empty cardboard box. It was just the right size for a swamp. Deniz looked at the box. It was deep and dark inside. He looked at his dinosaur. A big idea popped into his head.

"That's not a box," said Deniz. "That's a swamp! A sticky, gooey, squishy mud swamp!"

Up, up went the dinosaur, high over the box.

"Ready?" said Deniz. "One... two... three!"

Down went the dinosaur, right into the middle of the swamp.

"Splosh!" went Deniz's mouth. "Squish! Squelch! Splosh!"

The dinosaur landed with a plop. Or, well, the bottom of the box.

"Oh no!" said Deniz, wiggling the dinosaur. "I'm stuck! The mud is too sticky!"

Deniz wiggled the dinosaur left. He wiggled it right. He pulled it up a tiny bit. Deniz huffed and puffed for the dinosaur. But the pretend mud was very, very sticky. Sticky, sticky, sticky! It went slurp and slop and held on tight. The dinosaur would not come out.

"Help!" said Deniz for the dinosaur. "Help, help! I'm stuck in the swamp! Nobody can climb out of this gooey mud!"

Deniz looked around the room. He needed a rescue tool. Something strong. Something long. Something perfect.

Then he saw it. The small blanket was lying on the floor, soft and cozy.

"A jungle vine!" said Deniz. "A long, long jungle vine!"

Deniz picked up the blanket. He threw one end over the edge of the box. The blanket hung down, down, down into the box. It looked just like a vine in a big green jungle.

"Grab on!" said Deniz.

Deniz used his fingers to move the dinosaur's little feet up the blanket. Up, up, up they went. Little by little. Bit by bit. Careful now...

"Almost there!" said Deniz. "Climb, climb, climb!"

The dinosaur climbed and climbed. Up, up, up the soft blanket vine. Until—pop!—it reached the very top.

"Hooray!" cheered Deniz. "You did it! You climbed out of the sticky swamp!"

But where should the dinosaur go now? Deniz looked around again. He saw a sofa cushion waiting nearby.

"That's a safe island!" said Deniz.

He carried the dinosaur through the air, over the blanket, past the box, and set it gently down on the soft cushion. Deniz patted the cushion. It was a perfect safe island.

"Safe island!" said Deniz. "No sticky mud here! Just soft and cozy land!"

The dinosaur was safe and sound. Deniz picked it up and gave it a big, happy hug.

"ROOOOAR!" went Deniz, loud and happy, for his brave dinosaur.
