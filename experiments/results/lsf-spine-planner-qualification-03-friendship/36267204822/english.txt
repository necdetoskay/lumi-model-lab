The Big Red Circle

Deniz was drawing on the courtyard stones. He had a blue chalk in one hand and a yellow chalk in the other. He drew a long blue road, wriggling like a snake, all along his side. Then he went tap-tap-tap with the yellow chalk, making little dashes down the middle.

Zzzoom! went his pretend car.

Nearby, his friend Ada was drawing a road too. Her road curved the other way, bending around a pebble like it was a hill.

"Vroom-vroom!" said Ada, pushing her finger along her road.

The two of them hummed and drew, side by side in the warm sun.

Then Deniz stopped. His road needed something at the very end. A stop sign! A red stop sign, so the pretend cars would know when to stop.

He opened the chalk box near his knees. Red, red, where was the red?

Just as Deniz reached in, another hand reached in too. Ada's hand!

They both looked. At the bottom of the box sat one short piece of red chalk. Just one. It was only as long as Deniz's thumb.

"Red," said Deniz.

"Red," said Ada.

Both of them grabbed it at the same time.

"I need it for my stop sign," said Deniz.

"I need it for my fire truck," said Ada.

Deniz held one end. Ada held the other. The little red chalk sat between them, and nobody let go. They were stuck, like two dogs with one biscuit.

Deniz scrunched his nose. Then he looked at his blue road. Then he looked at Ada's road. And then he saw it — his road and Ada's road almost touched in the middle of the courtyard. Just a tiny gap left.

"Hey," said Deniz. "Our roads almost bump into each other."

Ada leaned over to look. "Ooooh."

"What if we draw the red part together?" said Deniz. "You hold one end. I hold this end. We make one big red circle right where the roads meet."

Ada nodded fast. "A really big one!"

They knelt down close, each holding one end of the short chalk. They pushed it round and round on the stones. Round and round and round. The chalk was so short that their fingers almost bumped, and they giggled. The red circle grew bigger and bigger — a little wobbly, like a pancake — until it sat right on top of both roads.

"There!" said Deniz. "One big red circle!"

Then they took turns with the same short piece. Ada drew four round red wheels on her fire truck. Nee-naw, nee-naw! Then Deniz drew a fat red stripe on his stop sign. Stop!

The red chalk was shorter now, small as a pebble.

Deniz and Ada sat back on their heels and looked. Two roads, one blue and one curvy, joined by one big wobbly red circle in the middle.

Deniz put the little red chalk back into the chalk box, right between them.

"Vroom," said Ada.

"Vroom-vroom," said Deniz, and the cars drove on.
