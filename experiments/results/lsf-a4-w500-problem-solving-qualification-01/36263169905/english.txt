The Ramp for the Little Truck

Deniz rolled his small toy truck across the courtyard stones. Clickety-click, clickety-click, went the wheels.

Ahead was a low wooden platform, just right for parking a truck. Deniz rolled the truck straight at it.

But the platform had a step. A tall, straight-up step. The truck's front wheels bumped it and stopped. The truck could not climb.

"Hmm," said Deniz.

He pushed the truck a little harder. The front wheels bumped the wooden edge. Bump. Then they slid back down to the stones. The truck tipped back on its back wheels.

Deniz stopped pushing. He did not push again.

He looked around instead. And there, leaning by the wall, was a flat piece of sturdy cardboard from a box. A big, flat rectangle.

Deniz picked it up. He set one end of the cardboard on top of the platform. He set the other end down on the stones. Now there was a slope — a long, gentle slide from the stones up to the platform.

"Let's try that," said Deniz.

He rolled the truck to the bottom of the cardboard. The wheels started up the slope. But halfway up, the cardboard slid! It slipped away from the platform edge and flattened down onto the stones. The truck rolled back down with it.

Deniz watched carefully. He saw exactly where the cardboard had moved. The top end had slipped off the platform.

He picked the cardboard up again. This time he pressed the top edge firmly, firmly against the platform edge. He pushed it until it sat snug, with no gap at all.

Then he found one more thing: a small wooden block, light enough for him to carry. He set it down at the bottom edge of the cardboard, right where the ramp met the stones. The block sat against the cardboard like a little foot, so it could not slide backward.

Now to test it. Deniz pressed the cardboard lightly with one finger. It did not move. It stayed snug and still.

"Good," said Deniz.

He put the truck at the bottom of the ramp. He rolled it slowly, slowly up. The wheels rumbled over the cardboard — rummmble, rummmble — climbing, climbing. The truck rolled over the top edge and onto the platform.

It was up!

Deniz rolled the truck across the platform, all the way to the far side. Then he turned it around and sent it gently back down the ramp. Down and down it went, rummmble, rummmble, onto the stones.

Then Deniz turned the truck around one more time. Up the ramp it went, rummmble, rummmble, the little wheels singing on the cardboard, all the way to the top.

He parked the truck on the platform. And below it, the cardboard ramp lay steady, held snug by the small wooden block, ready for the next ride.
