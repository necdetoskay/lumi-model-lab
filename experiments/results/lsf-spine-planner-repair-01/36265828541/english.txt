Vroom, Parked!

Deniz was in the courtyard, holding his small yellow toy truck high in the air. "Vroom, vroom!" he said. The truck's wheels spun round and round. The sun was warm and the courtyard was quiet.

Then Deniz saw the low wooden platform at the other end of the courtyard. It had one tall step at the front, like a little stairs.

"Truck," said Deniz, "that is where you should park. Right up on top!"

He set the truck down and pushed it toward the step. But the step was too tall. The truck bumped the wood and tipped back. It rolled all the way back to Deniz's feet.

"Oh no," said Deniz. "Too tall!"

He tried again. He lifted the truck and pushed it straight up the step. Push, push, push! But the step was still too tall. The truck tipped and rolled back down. Bump, bump, bump.

Deniz stood still and thought. Then he looked around. He tapped his chin with his finger. Hmm.

Near the courtyard wall, he saw a big flat piece of cardboard, sturdy and strong. And near the platform, he saw one small wooden block.

"Ah-ha!" said Deniz. "A ramp! Trucks love ramps!"

He ran to the cardboard and dragged it across the courtyard. Scritch, scritch, scritch, went the cardboard on the ground. It was hard work, but Deniz was strong.

He lifted one end of the cardboard onto the platform top. The other end stayed on the ground. It looked like a long slide — a truck slide!

But when Deniz let go... whoosh! The cardboard slid down flat on the ground.

"Hey!" said Deniz. "Stay up, ramp!"

He tried again. Up went the cardboard. He let go. Whoosh! Down it slid again.

Then Deniz looked at the small wooden block. He picked it up. It was just right for carrying.

"Block," said Deniz, "you can be the helper."

He set the block down against the bottom of the platform step, snug and tight. Then he laid the cardboard over the block and up onto the platform top.

Slowly, he let go.

The cardboard stayed! It leaned on the block and made a long, slanted ramp.

Deniz stepped back. He held the truck low, right at the bottom of the ramp.

"Ready, truck?" he whispered. "One... two... three!"

He pushed the truck fast, fast, fast up the cardboard. The wheels went rat-a-tat-tat. Up, up, up the ramp went the little yellow truck — and then it rolled right onto the platform top!

It rolled a little way and stopped.

Parked!

Deniz clapped his hands. "Vroom, parked!"

He climbed up the step and picked up his truck. Then he drove it in happy little circles on top of the platform. Round and round and round it went.

"Vroom! Vroom! Vroom!" said Deniz.

The little yellow truck drove its circles on the platform, and the cardboard ramp stayed leaning on its block, all ready for the next ride.
