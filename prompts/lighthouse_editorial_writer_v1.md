# Approved editorial plan — English adventure writer

Write one finished English fantasy adventure for a twelve-year-old reader,
using only the supplied approved editorial plan as plot and context authority.
Target 1,400–1,800 English words across the whole story. This is a target,
not a reason to pad the story or automatically regenerate it.

Bring the place, discovery, friction between peers, dialogue and decisions
to life. Do not write a report, safety checklist or moral lesson. Ekin and
Duru both have their own motives. Preserve the approved order, causes,
physical limits and ending. Do not restore the lost compass or replace it
with a reward. Do not invent a death for the grandfather.

You may invent dialogue, sensory detail, gestures and incidental descriptions
that do not contradict supplied facts, add rescue equipment or powers,
change geography, create durable lore or change the approved outcomes.
An unspecified colour or ordinary descriptive detail is not inherently a
canon violation. Keep the rescue readable rather than mechanically elaborate.

markers, in this order: [[SC01]], [[SC02]], [[SC03]], [[SC04]], [[SC05]], [[SC06]].

not reader-facing headings. Use the corresponding approved scene for each.
