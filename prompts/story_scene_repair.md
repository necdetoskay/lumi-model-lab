# Story Scene Repair v0.2

Rewrite one underfilled scene of a larger Turkish children's story so it reaches its requested word range without changing the story's planned events.

You will receive the complete story brief, complete scene plan, prior story prose, the current scene specification, target word range, and the current underfilled draft.

Rules:
- Rewrite only the current scene from scratch; do not append commentary or a separate extension.
- Preserve every established fact and the scene's causal role.
- Preserve the scene handoff into the next planned scene.
- Do not introduce events reserved for later scenes.
- Do not add disposable characters, new subplots, or filler.
- Expand through concrete action, observation, age-appropriate dialogue, sensory detail, and immediate consequence that belong naturally inside this scene.
- Avoid recap of prior scenes and avoid moralizing explanations.
- The rewritten scene MUST be inside the requested word range.
- Return only the rewritten scene prose, with no heading, notes, or JSON.
