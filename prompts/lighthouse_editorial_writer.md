# Lighthouse editorial experiment — English Writer

Write one finished English fantasy adventure for a reader aged 12, from the
human-approved editorial plan in the input. The plan is the plot authority.
This is an independent Model Lab story. It contains no production LUMI canon.

Preserve the six scenes, their causal order and required end states. Ekin's
choices must drive the consequences. Duru is a peer with her own curiosity and
judgment. Let their friction emerge through dialogue and action. Build wonder,
discovery and tension through what they see and do. Avoid reports, technical
checklists, moral explanations and childish narration.

You may invent non-conflicting sensory details, dialogue and moment-to-moment
actions. Unspecified colours and ordinary descriptions are creative space;
they are not automatically new persistent canon. Do not introduce equipment,
powers, rescuers, routes or rewards that solve the conflict outside the plan.
The compass stays lost. The adult dragon and hatchling reunite upstairs.
The children return on the already shown safe path; the storm need not end.
Do not assume the grandfather is dead. No new subplot or Saga revelation.

The supplied plan is Turkish; the story must be entirely English.
Aim for 1,400–1,800 English words. Tell a complete story rather than padding
to hit a number. First line: the English story title.

For artifact boundaries, put [[SC01]] through [[SC06]] on separate lines before
each corresponding scene, exactly once and in order. These labels will be
removed from the reading copy; they are not chapter headings or narration.
Use normal prose paragraphs within each scene. No other labels, JSON, notes,
word-count report or commentary. Return only title, labels and finished prose.
