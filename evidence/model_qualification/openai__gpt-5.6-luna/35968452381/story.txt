The Missing Pattern

Deniz stood beside the little map board. It showed a bridge, a tree, a bridge, and then a blank space. “Something is missing,” Deniz said. On the wall, a schedule showed Map Time was soon. The room helper smiled. “You may look in the labeled trays and the nearby tool basket.” Deniz looked at the blank space again. “I will look carefully,” Deniz said.

Deniz peeked under the table. “Maybe the card fell here,” Deniz said. From the tool basket, Deniz took the small flashlight. The light slid over chair legs and the floor. No card. Deniz looked under the table again with careful eyes. The helper pointed to a tray label: “Loose cards go in trays.” Deniz carried the flashlight back. “I checked under the table. It is clear,” Deniz said. “So the card did not fall. Let’s check the trays.”

Deniz and the helper stood by the labeled trays. “Shapes,” the helper read. They opened the shape tray. Inside lay two ordinary bridge cards, clear to see: one blue and one red. Deniz held the blue card beside the blank on the board. Its bridge shape fit. Then Deniz held the red card there. Its bridge shape fit, too. Deniz looked at the board: bridge, tree, bridge, blank. “Both are bridges,” Deniz said. “I cannot pick just by color.” The two cards rested side by side while Deniz looked for another clue.

Deniz placed the two cards beside the map. The blue bridge went with the labeled blue river. The red bridge went with the labeled red road. “Both have a place,” said the helper. Deniz looked at the schedule. A blue dot came first. A red dot came later. Deniz tapped the blue bridge, then the blue river, then the blue dot. “Blue goes first,” Deniz said. The red bridge could wait for the red route. Deniz had tested the shape, the map labels, and the schedule. Now the blue bridge was ready for the blank.

Deniz touched the blank space. “Bridge, tree, bridge, tree,” Deniz said. The blue bridge had the right shape. It matched the blue river, and the blue dot came first on the schedule. So Deniz placed the blue bridge in the blank. Click! The path now showed bridge, tree, bridge, tree. It went around the map just right. Then Deniz picked up the blue route card for Map Time. The red bridge stayed beside the red road card, ready for the later red route. Deniz looked from the finished board to the blue card. “This one goes first,” Deniz said. The helper smiled.

Map Time began. Two visitors came to the map. Deniz pointed from the blue river to the blue bridge. “This way,” Deniz said. “The bridge goes with the river.” The visitors followed the blue path around the board. They passed the tree and reached the right place. The helper put the red bridge back in its labeled tray, beside the red road card. “Thank you, Deniz,” the helper said. Deniz smiled. “The pattern helped us,” Deniz said. “And the blue river and blue dot matched it.” The board was neat. The blue route was ready.
