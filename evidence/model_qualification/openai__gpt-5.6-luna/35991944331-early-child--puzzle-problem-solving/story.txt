The Missing Pattern

Deniz stepped into the learning room. Maps lay in a map-marked place. Shells sat on a shelf with a shell picture. At a table lay a ruler, crayons, and tape. A route board showed: map, shell, map, shell, then an empty square. Nearby, a picture schedule showed little pictures. The space helper smiled. Deniz touched the empty square. “I can help,” Deniz said. “I will look carefully before I choose a card.”

Deniz pointed to the board. “Map, shell, map, shell,” Deniz said. “Shell next?” The guess made sense. Shell followed map twice. The helper touched the picture schedule. “Map, shell, map, shell, then tools,” the helper read aloud. Deniz pointed to the last picture. “Tools?” Deniz looked at the shell shelf, then at the tools table. The route ended at the tools table, not the shell shelf. “You noticed,” said the helper. Deniz’s shell guess no longer fit. Which tool belonged there?

Deniz followed the pictures. First, the map picture led to the map table. The helper pointed to its label. “Map,” the helper read. Deniz touched the map picture. Next, the shell picture led to the shell shelf. “Shell,” read the helper. Deniz pointed again. The pictures were not just decorations. They showed where to go. At the tools table, Deniz saw a ruler beside a ruler picture. A blank card outline sat nearby. The helper read the label. “Ruler.” Deniz touched the ruler. “Ruler goes here?”

Deniz stood by the route board. One finger touched map, shell, map, shell. “Map. Shell,” Deniz said. Then Deniz pointed to the last picture on the schedule. The helper read, “Tools.” Deniz pointed to the map table and shell shelf. “They match,” said the helper. At the tools table, Deniz touched the ruler picture, the real ruler, and the ruler-shaped outline. “Ruler,” Deniz said. “Map. Shell. Tools. Ruler.” The helper nodded. “Shall we try the ruler card?” Deniz smiled, picked up the card, and carried it to the board. “We can test.”

Deniz placed the ruler card in the empty space. The board now showed map, shell, map, shell, ruler. Deniz followed each picture. First came the map table. Then the shell shelf. Then the map table again. Then the shell shelf. The last picture led to the tools table, just as the helper had read on the schedule. Deniz pointed to the tools label, the ruler picture, and the ruler-shaped outline. A shell sat nearby, but Deniz left it there. Deniz picked up the ruler. “Ruler!” said Deniz. The helper smiled. “The board, schedule, places, and ruler all match.” The route was complete.

Deniz carried the ruler to the activity table. The helper unrolled a long paper strip. Together, they measured it. Deniz held one end. The helper moved the ruler along the strip. “Here,” said Deniz. “And here.” Small marks went across the paper.

Deniz pointed to the route board, then to the ruler. “We checked. It fit!” Deniz said.

“It did,” said the helper. “The route showed us the right tool.”

They put the ruler beside the strip and finished setting out the pattern pieces. Deniz smiled. The learning space was ready.
