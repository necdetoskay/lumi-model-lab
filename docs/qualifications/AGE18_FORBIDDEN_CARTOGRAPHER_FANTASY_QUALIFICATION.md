## Character

Leyra, age 18, junior boundary cartographer and ward-map apprentice.

She grew up in Greyfen beside the eastern ward-line and now works from Vellum Hall under Master Ilyan.
Her professional goal is to earn an independent map-seal license.

## World

The Veiled Marches use rule-bound cartographic magic.
Properly witnessed and sealed maps help stabilize real roads and borders.
An unverified map claim can therefore create practical and magical danger.

Key accessible locations:
- Vellum Hall
- Greyfen
- Ashen Gate

Foreshadow-only:
- The Nameless Vale

Locked:
- The Archive of Cinders

## Controlled premise

Title: The Road the Map Refused to Admit

During a routine re-survey, Leyra finds that Ashen Gate — legally sealed for more than a century —
is answering to a road the legal map says does not exist.

A private field mark associated with Master Ilyan may indicate prior survey activity, but it does
not prove motive, cause, suppression, or conspiracy.

First-episode evidence ceiling:
- may establish one bounded Ashen Gate anomaly;
- may earn forbidden_passage_observed;
- must not establish why the road returned;
- must not establish what lies in the Nameless Vale;
- must not establish other sealed roads are changing;
- must not establish Master Ilyan acted maliciously or knowingly hid a confirmed reopening.
