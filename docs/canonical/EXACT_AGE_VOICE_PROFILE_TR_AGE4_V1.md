# LUMI Exact-Age Voice Profile — Age 4 / Turkish v0.1

Status: EXPERIMENTAL
Purpose: positive craft guidance for Turkish stories read aloud to an exact-age-four child.

This profile complements the Story Failure Pattern Registry. The failure registry says what recurrent defects to avoid; this profile says what successful age-four prose should positively sound and behave like. It is not a developmental diagnosis and must not be treated as a rigid checklist.

## Core voice

Narration:
- concrete, immediately visualizable actions and objects
- short causal distance: what happens now matters more than abstract interpretation
- inner state is usually shown through speech, movement, pause, face, repetition, or play
- one image at a time; metaphors are concrete, playful, and easy to picture
- read-aloud rhythm matters more than literary sophistication

Child dialogue:
- usually short and direct
- spontaneous rather than carefully negotiated
- may repeat a word or structure when excited
- may jump from observation to play without explaining the transition
- may ask simple "why/where/what" questions without needing a polished hypothesis
- preferences can be blunt: "Hayır, bu benim.", "Ben de!", "Bunu istiyorum."
- future plans should normally be immediate and excited rather than formally conditional or negotiated
- imaginative claims can remain playful without an adult immediately correcting them

Caregiver:
- warm participant, not teacher, judge, narrator surrogate, or answer machine
- can wonder, laugh, mishear, join a game, make a small mistake, or admit not knowing
- does not need to praise every successful action
- does not translate every event into a lesson
- safety appears naturally through proximity/action when needed

Peer interaction:
- conflict and cooperation emerge through possession, imitation, turn-taking, shared action, curiosity, or play
- children do not need adult-style negotiation language
- a shared game can solve a social problem without stating the moral
- do not make a four-year-old summarize friendship, fairness, compromise, or future cooperation abstractly

## Positive transformations

Instead of:
"Deniz bir an düşündü, sonra başıyla onayladı."
Prefer behavior like:
"Deniz kamyona baktı. 'Peki,' dedi."

Instead of:
"Peki ben de bir şey getiririm. Belki bir çekici."
Prefer spontaneous language like:
"Ben de yarın çekici getiririm!"

Instead of:
"Onu ben de sürebilir miyim?"
Prefer, when character/context allows:
"Ben de sürsem?"

Instead of explaining:
"Deniz bu cevabı çok beğendi."
Show:
"Deniz kıkırdadı."

Instead of subtle adult-state analysis:
"Annesi gülmemek için kendini zor tuttu."
Show:
"Annesinin ağzı güldü."

These are behavior examples, not phrases to copy.

## Read-aloud heuristics

- avoid long chains of subordinate clauses
- prefer one action or image per breath unit
- vary dialogue tags; omit them when speaker is obvious
- sound effects must correspond to an action the child can understand
- repeated phrases should feel intentionally playful, not mechanically generated

## Success criteria

A successful age-four voice should make a parent feel they can read the text aloud without mentally rewriting sentences, while the child character remains recognizably impulsive, concrete, playful, and emotionally legible.

The profile must not force every four-year-old character to sound identical. Character Foundation, Origin, Saga, culture, language environment, temperament, and current emotional state remain authoritative inputs.
