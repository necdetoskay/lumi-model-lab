# Natural Author Voice Profile — Turkish v0.1

Status: EXPERIMENTAL

This layer is not an AI-detector evasion mechanism. Its purpose is editorial: reduce recurrent machine-like prose habits and produce cleaner professional fiction.

It complements, but does not replace:
- StoryContract / canon constraints
- exact-age positive voice profiles
- Story Failure Pattern Registry

Core principle:

**Do not explain what the reader can already infer from action or dialogue.**

The writer should trust scenes, tolerate small asymmetry, avoid making every detail meaningful, and finish on concrete character behavior rather than a generic emotional summary. Natural variation is preferred over deliberately inserted mistakes or artificial imperfection.

The machine-readable rules are authoritative for runtime compilation:
`configs/story_voice_profiles/natural_author_tr.v1.json`.
