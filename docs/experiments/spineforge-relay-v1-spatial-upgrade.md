`SpatialContractV1` is a versioned sidecar bound to the exact existing scene contract and StoryState. Historical model serialization and fingerprints stay unchanged. Its topology declares locations, directional/bidirectional edges, named passages, entity passage permissions and canonical location pointers. Its persistent state lives under `/world/spatial`.

Ordered operations locate newly observed entities, move entities through declared edges, activate/release a declared access constraint, or access a target. Movement cannot bypass a blocker. Only the bound blocking entity may release its posture, with a visible transition before access. Persistent constraints carry across scenes; scene boundaries never clear them. Canonical character/object/world locations must agree with spatial positions at entry and exit.

Writer and guard receive the identical sidecar. Each operation references a unique structured required-event predicate. The semantic guard still owns prose entailment: deterministic checks cannot establish that a quoted sentence means a movement or posture transition. Local checks enforce exact attribution, direct-observation provenance, unique non-overlapping anchors in operation order, topology, state persistence and source/exit fingerprints. Residual guards audit unlisted movements and meaning ambiguities.
