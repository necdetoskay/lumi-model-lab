# Editoryal süreklilik ve telif korumaları

- BAS-B01: 2013 and 2014 episodic DLC are two separately versioned works, not two sections of the 2013 base campaign.
- BAS-B02: 2013 Episode One playable Rapture Booker is a divergent Comstock variant, not the same body as Infinite base Booker.
- BAS-B03: Anna dies in this diverging portal event while original Infinite main branch Anna loses a finger; no same-body simultaneous outcome.
- BAS-B04: Elizabeth returned to Rapture in Episode Two is mortal and has lost prior tear and near-omniscience powers; no retroactive full-power rescue.
- BAS-B05: Paris opening is subjective presentation, not an independently verified new historical planet or ordinary objective flashback.
- BAS-B06: Booker speaking to Elizabeth in Episode Two is a mental guidance representation, not physical resurrection of Rapture Comstock.
- BAS-B07: Rapture Fontaine store isolated and later raised via physical device and quantum particle; world access changes require causal evidence.
- BAS-B08: Suchong materials and Atlas control-code link are reported by community plot and later guide; no independent audited video replay.
- BAS-B09: Giving Atlas a code is not identical to Jack arriving and making all 2007 original BioShock decisions.
- BAS-B10: BioShock 2007 branching epilogues not resolved as unique absolute canon by the Elizabeth hope narrative.
- BAS-B11: Creator and original publisher descriptions confirm release, playable character and stealth; do not treat them as primary proof for every final plot reveal.
- BAS-B12: Contemporary GameFAQs and wiki plot summaries differ in confidence; fan synopsis must remain community-level.
- BAS-B13: Optional audio recordings depend on character actually collecting or hearing them; static metadata does not grant omniscient knowledge.
- BAS-B14: No reused same physical body across distinct alternative worlds without evidence, and no DLC event leakage into old v0.10–v0.12 files.
- BAS-B15: Do not reproduce copyrighted dialogue, unique character scenes, visual layouts or commercial source content in generated stories.
- BAS-B16: No model calls, no story quality trials, no production/Harness updates; research only.
