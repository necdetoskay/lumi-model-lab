# Araştırma süreklilik/telif/kesinlik korumaları

1. Only original BioShock Infinite 2013 base campaign, no Burial at Sea episodes, Clash in Clouds, later remastered supplement.
2. World ID must separate Booker baptism-refusing variant from Comstock accepting variant; do not merge physical histories.
3. Anna and Elizabeth are names of one biological person after transfer in plot account; not two independently cloned daughters.
4. Older Elizabeth from future vision is not young Elizabeth's present belief state and is not a second co-temporal mother.
5. Lutece counterparts from different worlds are not automatically biologically born twins or one shared physical body.
6. Chen Lin dead in encountered world A but alive in world B; no retroactive resurrection.
7. Booker martyr account in world C differs from present visiting Booker, whose alive state does not contradict world C local history.
8. Minor symbolic player choices can alter local visuals but not necessarily main story ending; keep choice_impact_scope distinct.
9. Combat Tear objects do not equal entire protagonist transitioning world; model tactical summon vs full world migration separately.
10. Cross-world item access and persistent inventory need world-scoped object ownership, never a flat universal inventory.
11. 2013 developer interview describes design goals and limits, not an independent technical audit of code or all scenes.
12. Player-written 2013 walkthroughs and contemporary plot summaries can contain inferences; source provenance always qualified.
13. Optional Voxophones require acquisition/listening for corresponding character knowledge; metadata alone does not propagate facts.
14. Songbird ability and Siphon active state constrain Elizabeth's access; do not retrospectively give all powers at opening.
15. Older Elizabeth future warning is a conditional future-world encounter, not unique universal destiny proven in every branch.
16. Booker/Comstock branch intervention is characters' intended strategy, not rigorous mathematical proof of universal erasure.
17. Postcredit Anna appearance deliberately unresolved; do not infer every alternative world has identical aftermath.
18. Rapture short visit in finale is not permission to import Burial at Sea DLC events or retroactively rewrite BioShock 2007 original canon.
19. World events, speaker claims, story interpretation, and quality-effect claims are different evidence categories.
20. No verbatim dialogue, franchise characters, distinct level puzzles or copyrighted setpieces copied into generated stories.
21. Research only; no paid model calls, story generation, story quality A/B tests or LUMI production/Harness integration.

## Editoryal belirsizlikler

- BI-BND-01: The Rapture cameo exists in Infinite base finale but Burial at Sea Episodes 1/2 and Clash in the Clouds are out of scope.
- BI-BND-02: Bird/cage, raffle and Slate choices differ locally but no multiple major endings in 2013 game; community reports are not multi-run independent audits.
- BI-BND-03: First Chen Lin death is not reversed in original world when alive alternate variant found.
- BI-BND-04: Booker and Comstock alternative baptism variants: separate physical/knowledge states; do not merge life events.
- BI-BND-05: Robert and Rosalind Lutece must not be treated as literal biological twins without separate evidence.
- BI-BND-06: Anna name changes to Elizabeth after cross-world transfer; her pinky displacement is one reported event, not full person replication.
- BI-BND-07: Ending drown attempt and postcredit Anna scene do not prove absolute universe erasure or single final survival history.
- BI-BND-08: 2013 Levine interview about Tears and variables supports design intent not exhaustive gameplay traces.
- BI-BND-09: Voxophone clues depend on when collected; Columbia historical records are not automatically known to Booker.
- BI-BND-10: No E3 promo or discarded demo scenes accepted as actual 2013 gameplay events.
