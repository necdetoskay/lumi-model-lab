# Sürüm, kurmaca ve fiziksel süreklilik sınırları

1. Only original BioShock 2 main 2010 campaign and separate 2010 Minervas Den original story DLC; Protector Trials and Remastered-only content excluded.
2. Delta, Sigma and the Big Daddies are different subjects; being a Big Daddy is role/body type not equal identity.
3. Prologue 1958 vs main 1968 vs first BioShock 1960; do not infer exact Minerva to main campaign hour/order.
4. Main campaign and Minerva story have separate inventories, actor perspectives, mission records and ending state.
5. Delta-Eleanor biological bond depends on physical state; temporarily stopping heartbeat is not verified permanent death.
6. Character spare/kill choices for Grace, Stanley and Gil separate from all Little Sister save/harvest decisions.
7. Child Eleanor can model Delta behavior, but do not merge her mind or agency with Delta; observer knowledge is conditional.
8. Prima licensed guide gives qualitative ending category; avoid guessing all scenario-specific condition combinations without direct playthrough.
9. Gil old audio diaries are historical claims not current requests by mutated self.
10. Sinclair transformed by force, no free-will role shift automatically inferred.
11. Sigma physically Charles Milton Porter; Porter-voiced radio guidance is machine Thinker emulation, not second physical Porter.
12. Pearl voice emulation not human resurrection; no assumption digital clone proves subjective consciousness continuity.
13. Thinker powerful computation not perfect prophecy; Wahl prediction assertion is antagonist belief rather than law.
14. The city devices, doors and security control have state and access rights, no ungrounded location or item jumps.
15. Audio diary evidence is optional where marked, and player knowledge acquired on listening not omniscient.
16. Only contemporary official Xbox description and current official 2K overview are primary; tertiary endgame details remain lower confidence.
17. Do not reproduce copyrighted character names, lines, level geometry or scene expression in generated stories.
18. Research only: no paid models, story-generation, A/B evaluation, LUMI production/Story Harness integration. Quality not evaluated.
