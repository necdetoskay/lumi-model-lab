2. Earlier Prototype 2009 recorded as distinct prior work. First-game gameplay viewpoint is not the second protagonist knowledge or moral endorsement.
3. Heller belief Maya is dead does not turn actual Maya world state into dead; revelation is a knowledge update.
4. Mercer original identity is a viral re-creation: memory and bodily continuity separate; sequel antagonistic role must not prove true subject continuity.
5. The developer interview documents why creators chose new hero vs former hero, not a complete in-world explanation for Mercers motives.
6. Reported Penn Station second outbreak evidence is not identical to original first-game release; temporal cause / agent must be differentiated.
7. Guerra/Athena/Dana information relay requires known communications; alias revelation is not evidence an additional new character exists.
8. Evolved / Koenig / Galloway roles are not the same individual or permanently fixed allegiance.
9. Blackwatch and Gentek are institutions with independent goals; Heller may infiltrate both but their knowledge cannot be pooled freely.
10. Vaccine/Whitelight public message differs from actual reported viral effect; treat community source as lower confidence.
11. Ending claims are supported by community walkthrough and tertiary plot rather than independent primary gameplay capture.
12. Prototype city zones indicate narrative progression in 2012, not free inference of exact constant infection levels.
13. No copied character names/scenes/dialogue into novel works: only generalized mechanisms and evidence provenance.
14. No paid model or story trials, LUMI production integration, or storyboard/story harness calls. quality_effect stays not_evaluated.
