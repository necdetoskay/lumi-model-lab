# Yayınlanan kanon / süreklilik / fikir ayrımı

1. Classic Core Design 1996-2003 is a separate research continuity from Survivor 2013-2018 and Legend/Anniversary/Underworld 2006-2008.
2. Remastered releases are editions, not additional main story units; expansions need explicit different content records.
3. Published-game event vs historical lore vs retrospective framing vs development plan must remain separately typed.
4. Chronicles narrated memory may be selective; frame chronology, historical chronology and release chronology must not be merged.
5. The Last Revelation finale is presumed death/uncertain survival, not definitive recorded death.
6. Angel of Darkness writer/designer interviews contain cut/unreleased plans; these are not gameplay canon.
7. Authorial intention and observed in-game evidence are distinct proof categories.
8. Artifact ownership, historical holders and location changes require explicit transition evidence.
9. Older mechanic cross-links indicate resemblance not semantic identity or increased story quality.
10. Do not copy Tomb Raider characters, proprietary puzzles, locations, written dialogue or other original protected expression into new narratives.
11. Zero story-generation or A/B tests, zero paid model calls and no LUMI runtime/production change.
