# Süreklilik, agency, telif ve kanıt bariyeri

1. Only original BioShock 2007 main campaign. Exclude DLC, Challenge Rooms, Remastered-exclusive content, sequels and adaptations.
2. Actor beliefs are not objective events. Atlas/Fontaine alias and claimed family are separate narrative knowledge states.
3. Would You Kindly is a direct copyrighted story cue; extract its conditional obedience abstraction without replicating dialogue in new stories.
4. Player tactical options and mandated Ryan confrontation are not equivalent types of narrative agency.
5. Environmental physics must be consistent; evidence from Ken Levine interviews about intended design does not prove every runtime system outcome.
6. Audio diary contents enter protagonist knowledge only on collection/listening and must not be treated as omniscient narration.
7. Little Sister Rescue/Harvest is a player choice. Choice totals are separate from one specific scripted story beat.
8. Exact ending numeric threshold is disputed by some player sources; keep null until direct gameplay evidence and version distinction audited.
9. Rescue and harvest epilogues are mutually exclusive outcomes. Never merge as one timeline without scenario context.
10. Tenenbaum's partial conditioning suppression and later Lot 192 treatment are separate biological state changes.
11. Cost and moral consequence of ADAM economy should not be flattened to equal numerical value, per contemporary developer discussion.
12. Big Daddy appearance, smell and voice prerequisites are distinct; disguise is not the same as changing personal identity.
13. Source provenance: published developer interviews are design intent; fan-written walkthroughs show gameplay report; neither is raw game-file extraction.
14. Research-only. Do not claim story quality improvement; no story generation, paid models or LUMI production integration.
15. Respect copyright: only conceptual abstract mechanisms; no lengthy verbatim dialogue or novel scenes copied into future stories.
